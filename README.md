An invented passport spread for a country that does not exist: the Republic of Kaldoria. The left page is the data page of Mara Ilsa Vey — portrait, MRZ with valid ICAO check digits, issued 12 FEB 2024 in Aldhaven, expiring 12 FEB 2034. The right page is the visa page: nine entry and exit stamps from three neighbours (Ostrovia, Meridia, Tarnovia), a Tarnovia e-visa, and the entry-fee table that shows how Kaldoria treats its neighbours — visa-free, visa on arrival for 25 kaldor, e-visa for 40 kaldor. Every stamp falls between issue and expiry.
